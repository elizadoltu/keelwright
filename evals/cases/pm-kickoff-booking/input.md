# Climbing gym bookings

I'm the project manager at Vertical, a climbing gym in Cluj. We want members to book a slot online instead of calling the front desk: pick a day and a 2-hour slot, see how many places are left (the gym holds 40 people at once), and get a confirmation email. Staff need a page to see today's bookings and check people in at the door.

What we don't know yet:
- The launch date depends on next quarter's budget approval — I'll know in about a month.
- The cancellation rules (how late someone can cancel, what happens on a no-show) aren't decided — that's for Mara, our business analyst, to work out with the owners.
- Whether we can read our existing member list from the old PHP membership app, or have to import it — our developer has to look at it.

Members use their phones for almost everything. Romanian only for now.
