# Project idea: personal website

## What I want

One website that contains two different landing pages, each presenting a different side of me:

1. **Personal page**: who I am professionally. It includes:
   - a short description about me
   - my projects
   - my skills
   - a contact section
   - a blog where I write posts

2. **Ski instructor page**: me as a ski instructor, with my experience and teaching style.

Both pages are part of the same website, but each one should work on its own. Someone looking for a ski instructor shouldn't have to go through my developer profile to find what they need, and the other way round.

I already have a personal website with a short description about me, my projects, my skills and a contact section. This new site replaces it, and its content can be reused.

It's a personal project, built and maintained by me alone.

## What matters to me

- Both pages should be easy to find in search, each for its own audience.
- It should look good and work well on phones.
- It should be fast and easy for me to maintain. Writing a new blog post or adding a project should not feel like a chore.
- I'd rather keep it simple than build features I don't need yet.

## What I haven't decided yet

- How the two pages relate: separate addresses on the same site, or something else, and how visitors move between them.
- Whether both pages share one look and feel, or each has its own style.
- Where the blog posts live and how I write them.
- How people contact me: a form, email, social links, or a mix. And whether it's the same for both pages.
- Which languages the site should be in.

## Out of scope for now

- Booking lessons online.
- Online payments.
- User accounts or logins for visitors.
