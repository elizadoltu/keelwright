# Project idea: Streaks

A habit tracker app for iPhone and Android phones. People add the habits they want to keep (read 20 minutes, no sugar, walk 8,000 steps), tick them off each day and see their current streak and a monthly calendar.

It has to work without a connection — people tick habits off on the underground — and what they did offline must not be lost. With an account, habits sync between a person's phone and tablet.

A daily reminder at a time the person chooses. Free for now; a paid tier may come later, not in the first release.

We're two developers who build web apps in TypeScript and React. The app goes to the App Store and Google Play.
