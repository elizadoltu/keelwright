# Trail-run club site — notes

We're a running club of about 60 people in Brasov. We want a small website:

- what the club is, where and when we meet (Tuesday and Saturday mornings), and the routes we run;
- a calendar of upcoming races we go to together;
- a form to ask to join, which emails the two organisers.

No accounts, no payments. The organisers update the race calendar a few times a month and aren't developers. Romanian first, English second.
