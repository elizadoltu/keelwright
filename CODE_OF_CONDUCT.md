# Code of Conduct

KeelWright follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space — is expected to follow it.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and don't harass, insult or exclude anyone.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the maintainer at **<contact email — to be added before the public release>**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
